About This Webpage

Welcome to my teaching and academic support homepage. This website provides course materials and academic resources for physics courses.

I am currently a senior student in the Department of Optics and Optical Engineering, School of Physical Sciences, enrolled in 2023. Since 2025, I have served as a peer instructor in the School of Physical Sciences’ Peer-Assisted Learning Program, providing course guidance and learning support for students.

My teaching experience includes courses such as Mechanics A/H, Electromagnetism A/H and Optics A. I also welcome discussions and exchanges related to Quantum Mechanics, Quantum Optics, Fundamentals of Quantum Information, Introductory Electrodynamics, Introductory Statistical Physics and Intermediate Classical Optics.

The teaching support for Mechanics A/H and Optics A is planned to continue through Fall 2027, and Electromagnetism A/H through Spring 2028. More courses and related resources will be added to this website in the future.

Feel free to contact me via e-mail: yftong@mail.ustc.edu.cn .

• Fundmentals of Modern Optics 038008
Course taken: Spring 2025
This is a required course designed specifically for students in the Department of Optics and Optical Engineering, providing an introduction to optical researches. It is also one of the top three courses I have taken during my undergraduate studies, and I highly recommend that sophomores interested in optics consider taking it early. This collection of conceptual questions is intended to make up for the lack of a suitable textbook. After completing each chapter, it can be used to review the key ideas, and it may also be helpful for students wanting to explore beyond the depth covered in Optics A. The main reference is Max Born and Emil Wolf's Principles of Optics, 7th edition.
📄 Conceptual Questions for Modern Optics (PDF)
• Quantum Optics PHYS5255P
Course taken: Spring 2026
This is one of the core graduate-level courses in the Department of Optics. The material mainly follows the first nine chapters of Scully’s classic textbook, with additional material from several other textbooks.
